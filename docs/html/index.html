Build The Moment.
Invite The Guess.

  1. Clip A Basketball Shot

    Keep enough of the setup to make the prediction interesting. Save the outcome for the reveal.

  2. Make Viewers Commit

    “Make or miss?” Answer cards and a countdown invite a prediction before the play finishes.

  3. Reveal & Start A Conversation

    Replay the outcome. Invite viewers to share their guess, compare reactions, or watch again.

This is the intended content format, not a claim of measured engagement lift. This interactive example uses an illustration; the final demo will show the actual shot footage and portrait preview.

Current Boundaries

Ball and hoop detections can be sparse. Player tracks can fragment or swap. Highlight suggestions require review; they do not establish official statistics or verified athlete identities.

The prototype has been exercised on short clips, not validated as full-game coverage. Crew output can be metadata-only. This project page runs independently of the analysis app.
