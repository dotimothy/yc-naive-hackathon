A Video’s Lifecycle

What runs where
StepExecutionResult
1. Check & ImportLocal API validates input, probes media, and records the source hash.A game record linked to registered media.
2. AnalyzePlayer and basketball models share a decode loop. Tracking and camera boundaries organize observations.Timestamped tracks, object observations, and analysis reports.
3. Export CandidatesLocal heuristics select moments. FFmpeg renders clips and checks decoding, bounds, duration, and alignment.Source-linked candidate clips. Media QA does not establish a made or missed shot.
4. Coordinate The CrewThe dispatcher builds source-bound evidence and starts Naive specialist sessions.A validated package of clip IDs, captions, reasons, and uncertainty notes.
5. Render The FormatLocal rendering uses the selected clip and quiz or storytelling configuration.A captioned portrait story or a replay with a prediction question and reveal.
6. Publish If EnabledThe publisher checks policy and provenance, saves requests, and reconciles provider receipts.A confirmed post, a pending delivery, or a recorded error.

Outreach belongs to the CEO’s team but is separate from the five video-analysis specialists. AgentMail credentials stay in the local worker; Naive receives bounded message and catalog data. Email requests do not enable social publishing. Live intake requires a separately authenticated worker and an online app for preview links. Local integration checks and a live metadata-only outreach recommendation have passed. Provider rate limits may delay requests; this check did not send an email or publish a post.

Data & Provenance

Source Identity
Hashes bind jobs, clips, and crew packages to their input footage.
Time Coordinates
Clip boundaries and observations refer to the original source in milliseconds.
Persistent State
SQLite holds analysis and review records. The Naive integration uses a separate workflow store and local journals.
Media Storage
Uploads, model weights, and generated videos stay in ignored local storage rather than Git.

Control & Observability

Live Updates
The film room receives analysis updates. The crew dashboard polls its integration feed for sessions, tools, messages, and handoffs.
Cancellation
Persistent batch controls stop queued work and request cancellation of active work. Completed results and existing posts are retained.
Publishing Policy
Enablement, deadlines, cancellation, and saved request identity govern submission. External delivery errors remain visible.
Warnings
Dismissal hides current banners in the browser; error history remains available.
